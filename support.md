# Tally Support

Need help with Tally, or found a problem? Email **[versettifinance@gmail.com](mailto:versettifinance@gmail.com)** and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**  
No. Tally works without sign-in, and everything stays on your iPhone.

**How do I scan a receipt?**  
Tap **+** → **Scan receipt**, photograph the receipt, check the details, and tap **Save**.

**Why is a field highlighted amber after a scan?**  
The app wasn't sure about that value. Check it and correct it if needed before saving.

**How are foreign-currency amounts converted?**  
Each entry keeps its original currency. Totals use the Bank of Canada daily exchange rate.

**How do I delete my data?**  
Open **Settings → Delete all data**, or delete the app.
