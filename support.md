# Wealthline Support

Need help with Wealthline, or found a problem? Email **versettifinance@gmail.com** and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Wealthline works with no sign-in and no registration — everything stays on your iPhone.

**How do I add a transaction?**
Tap the **+** button and choose to enter it manually or scan a receipt.

**How do I scan a receipt?**
Tap **+ → Scan Receipt**, photograph the receipt or choose one from your library, check the details Wealthline extracted, and save.

**How do I import a bank or credit card statement?**
Tap **+ → Import Statement**, then choose a PDF or CSV file (up to 10 at once). Wealthline detects the institution and account and parses the transactions automatically; importing the same statement twice is detected and skipped.

**Why is a field highlighted after a scan or import?**
Wealthline wasn't fully confident about that value. Check it and correct it if needed before saving.

**How are foreign-currency amounts converted?**
Each transaction keeps its original currency. Totals use the Bank of Canada's daily exchange rate.

**How do I turn on Face ID / Touch ID lock?**
Go to **More → Settings** and turn on **Require Face ID / Touch ID**.

**How do I delete my data?**
Open **Settings → Delete all data**, or delete the app.
