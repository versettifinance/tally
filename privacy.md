# Privacy Policy for Tally

**Effective:** September 22, 2026  
**Publisher:** Versetti Inc., Ontario, Canada

**Summary:** We don't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

## What the app stores
The entries, budgets, tags, notes and receipt photos you create. They are stored only on your device, and we have no access to them.

## Camera
The camera is used only when you choose to scan a receipt. The photo and its text are processed on your device and saved with the entry you create.

## AI features
Receipt reading and question answering happen on your device, using Apple Intelligence where available or the app's built-in reader. Your data is not sent to any AI service.

## Network requests
When you record an entry in a foreign currency, the app requests public exchange rates from the Bank of Canada. That request contains only currency codes and dates, not personal information.

## No tracking
The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or third-party tracking, and does not sell or share personal information.

## Deleting your data
Use Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. Both remove everything the app stored.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information.

## Changes
If a future version adds features that send data off your device (for example, optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated and the app will ask for your permission first.
