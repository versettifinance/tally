# Privacy Policy for Wealthline

**Effective:** October 3, 2026
**Publisher:** Versetti Inc., Ontario, Canada

**Summary:** Wealthline does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud Backup, in your own private iCloud. We cannot see it.

## What the app stores
Your transactions, accounts, budgets, tags, goals, notes, and receipt photos are created and stored on your device. We have no access to them, and they are never uploaded to a server we control. Receipts are kept until you delete them.

## iCloud Backup (optional)
If you turn on iCloud Backup, Wealthline copies your data to your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), using the Apple Account signed in on your iPhone. Only your devices signed in to that Apple Account can read it; Versetti Inc. cannot access it. This lets your data come back if you reinstall the app or set up a new iPhone. Storage is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and counts toward your iCloud storage. You can turn iCloud Backup off at any time in Settings, and you can delete the backed-up data from your iPhone's iCloud settings.

## Camera and Photo Library
The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to scan a receipt. The merchant, date, and amount are extracted entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework, and saved with the entry you create.

## Bank statement import
When you import a bank or credit card statement (PDF or CSV), the file is parsed entirely on your device to detect the institution, account, and transactions. Statement files and their contents are never uploaded to a server we control.

## AI features
Receipt reading, categorization, and the AI assistant run on your device, using Apple Intelligence where your iPhone supports it, or the app's own built-in logic otherwise. Your financial data is not sent to any external AI service.

## Notifications
If you turn on reminders, Wealthline schedules local notifications on your iPhone (for example, a daily reminder to log spending or a bill reminder). They are created on your device; no notification content is sent to us.

## Widgets and Siri
Home Screen widgets and Siri shortcuts read your data on your device to show totals or log a transaction. Nothing is sent to us.

## Face ID / Touch ID
The optional App Lock feature uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Biometric verification happens entirely inside your device's Secure Enclave; Wealthline never has access to your raw biometric data.

## Network requests
Wealthline does not send your data to Wealthline's servers or to any third party, and it does not download exchange rates or other financial data. The app connects to the network only for:
- **iCloud Backup**, if you turn it on (Apple CloudKit, described above).
- **Address suggestions and maps** for the optional address field on a transaction, provided by Apple Maps (MapKit). The address text you type is sent to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Wealthline does not use your device's location.
- **Bank logos**, loaded from logo.dev. Only the bank's public website domain (for example, td.com) is sent, never your account details or transactions.
- **Card images** shown when you choose a credit card, loaded from the card issuer's public website. Only the image address is requested.

## No tracking
Wealthline has no Wealthline accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking.

## Deleting your data
Use Settings → Delete All Data, or delete the app. If iCloud Backup is on, also remove Wealthline's data from iCloud in your iPhone's Settings (your name → iCloud → Manage Storage).

## Children
Wealthline is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes
If a future version adds a feature that sends data off your device (for example, an optional bank-connection feature), this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for your permission before that feature is turned on.
